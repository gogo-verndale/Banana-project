## Pricing & Order Limits

- Base Pricing: The pricing model is based on an average cost of $0.10 per individual banana.
- Unit Price: Consequently, the standard price for one "Bunch of 12 Bananas" will be approximately $1.20 (12 * $0.10). This price may be subject to minor fluctuations based on market conditions but will serve as the baseline. Volume discounts for larger orders may be implemented.
- Order Volume: Customers can place orders ranging from a minimum of 1 bunch (12 bananas) up to a maximum of 1000 bunches (12000 bananas) per single transaction. The system must enforce this limit.
- Maximum Order Value (Approx): Based on the $1.20/bunch price, the maximum value for a single order would be approximately $1200 (1000 bunches * $1.20/bunch).
