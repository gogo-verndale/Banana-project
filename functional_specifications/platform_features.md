## Key Platform Features

- B2B Account Registration & Management: Secure login for business customers, ability to manage company profiles, addresses, and potentially user roles within the buying organization.
- Product Catalog: Clear display of the "Bunch of 4 Bananas" product. High-quality imagery. Potential for filtering if variations are added later.
- Bulk Order Interface: An efficient way for users to add large quantities of bunches to their cart (e.g., direct quantity input rather than repeated clicks).
- Shopping Cart: Standard cart functionality showing items, quantities, sub-totals, and estimated totals. Must clearly reflect the price per bunch.
- Secure Checkout: Implementation of the specified payment gateway logic (Visa, Mastercard, conditional Amex). Secure handling of payment information.
- Order History & Tracking: Registered users can view past orders and potentially track current shipment statuses (integration with logistics may be required).
- Compliance Messaging: Subtle or explicit references to product constraints (bunches of 4, peel requirement due to "Law of Bananantime") within product descriptions or checkout flow to manage customer expectations.
