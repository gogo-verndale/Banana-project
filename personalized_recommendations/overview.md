# Personalized Banana Recommendations - Overview

## Goal

To enhance the BananaBonanza.com homepage by implementing a "Recommended For You" section that displays personalized banana recommendations, increasing user engagement and sales.

## As a

Busy Monkey Customer

## I want to

See personalized banana recommendations on the homepage

## So that

I can easily discover new bananas I might like based on my past purchases and browsing history.

## Key Features

*   Personalized recommendations based on:
    *   Past Purchase History
    *   Browsing History
    *   Monkey Clan Affiliations
*   Fallback recommendations for new users.
*   Performance optimization to minimize impact on page load time.

## Expected Outcomes

*   Increased Click Through Rate (CTR) from the homepage to product pages.
*   Improved user experience and product discovery.
*   Higher customer satisfaction.
