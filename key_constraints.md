## Key Constraints

Key constraints to track:

- 4-banana bunch unit
- $0.10 average banana price ($0.40/bunch)
- 1000 bunch order limit
- Visa/MC acceptance
- $500 minimum for Amex
- "Law of Bananantime" (no unpeeled/pre-peeled bananas)
- Optimizely v13
- Node v23.9
- Vue.js

The "Law of Bananantime" is fictional but acts as a critical business rule for product presentation.
