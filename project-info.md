# Project Information: B2B Banana Hub

## Overview

The B2B Banana Hub is an e-commerce platform specializing in the wholesale distribution of bananas. It aims to streamline the ordering process for businesses requiring bulk banana supplies, providing a stable, scalable, and secure platform for transactions.

## Key Features

-   **B2B Account Management:** Secure registration and management for business customers.
-   **Product Catalog:** Focused on the "Bunch of 4 Bananas" unit.
-   **Bulk Order Interface:** Efficient interface for large quantity orders.
-   **Secure Checkout:** Supports Visa, Mastercard, and Amex (conditional).
-   **Order History & Tracking:** Registered users can view past orders and track shipments.

## Business Requirements

-   **Target Audience:** Retail grocery chains, restaurants, catering companies, and other food service providers.
-   **Core Product:** Bananas sold exclusively in bunches of 4, adhering to the "Law of Bananantime".
-   **Business Objectives:** Establishing a dominant online channel for B2B banana sales and capturing a significant market share.

## Functional Specifications

-   **Pricing:** Based on an average cost of $0.10 per banana ($0.40 per bunch).
-   **Order Limits:** Minimum of 1 bunch, maximum of 1000 bunches per transaction.
-   **Payment Processing:** Visa and Mastercard accepted for all orders; American Express accepted for orders over $500.00.

## Technical Specifications

-   **Platform:** Optimizely Content Cloud & Commerce Cloud (Version 13). 
-   **Frontend:** Vue.js.
-   **Build Environment:** Node.js version 23.9.
-   **Architecture:** Headless or hybrid-headless, with Optimizely as the backend and Vue.js for the frontend.

## Key Constraints

-   **Product:** 4-banana bunch unit only.
-   **Pricing:** $0.10 average banana price ($0.40/bunch).
-   **Order Limits:** Maximum 1000 bunches.
-   **Payment:** Visa/MC acceptance, $500 minimum for Amex.
-   **Law of Bananantime:** No unpeeled/pre-peeled bananas.

## Quality and Grading

-   **Ripeness Levels:** Potential future consideration for offering bananas at specific ripeness levels, with clear definitions, quality control, and separate SKUs.
