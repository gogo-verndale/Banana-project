## Ripeness Levels

Introducing ripeness levels adds complexity. Here's how we'd approach it:

Grading Consistency:

- Clear Definitions: Establish objective criteria for each ripeness level (e.g., "Green": Mostly green peel, firm; "Ready-to-Eat": Mostly yellow, slight green tips, firm but yielding; "Baking-Ready": Yellow with brown spots, soft). These definitions would need visual guides and training for warehouse staff.
- Quality Control: Implement a dedicated QC process where incoming bananas are sorted and graded based on these definitions upon arrival and potentially again before packing. This requires skilled staff and appropriate storage conditions to manage ripening.

Inventory Management:

- Separate SKUs: Each ripeness level (still sold in bunches of 4) would need its own SKU in Optimizely (e.g., BANANA-BUNCH4-GREEN, BANANA-BUNCH4-READY, BANANA-BUNCH4-BAKING).
- Dynamic Inventory: Inventory levels for each ripeness SKU would need careful management, as bananas naturally ripen. This might involve forecasting, controlled ripening rooms, or accepting a certain level of fluctuation/potential stock-outs for specific levels.

Managing Customer Expectations:

- Clear Labelling: Product pages must clearly show the selected ripeness level with accurate descriptions and representative photos.
- Disclaimer: Include a disclaimer stating that ripeness is graded upon packing, and minor variations can occur due to transit time and conditions. Manage expectations that it's a guideline, not an exact science guaranteed upon delivery.
- Lead Time: Orders for specific ripeness levels might require slightly longer lead times to ensure availability or proper grading.
