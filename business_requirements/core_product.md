## Core Product & Catalog

Sole Product: Bananas.

Sales Unit: The only unit available for purchase is a pre-packaged "Bunch of 4 Bananas". Individual bananas cannot be purchased. All SKUs and inventory management will revolve around this 4-banana bunch unit.

Product State: In accordance with the "Law of Bananantime", the platform is strictly prohibited from selling or distributing unpeeled bananas that have been artificially separated from their bunch or pre-peeled for the customer. All bananas must be sold within their natural peel, grouped in the mandatory 4-banana bunch format. This regulation must be clearly communicated or inherently enforced through the product offering. No "peeled banana" product options shall exist.

Potential Variations (Future Consideration): While initially launching with a standard Cavendish banana type, future iterations might include options for organic bunches or specific ripeness levels (e.g., "Green," "Ready-to-Eat," "Baking-Ready"), still adhering to the 4-banana bunch rule.

### Law of Bananantime

Core Principle: The "Law of Bananantime" is fundamentally about preserving the natural integrity and presentation of the banana until it reaches the end consumer's preparation stage. It prohibits the supplier (us) from altering the banana's natural state (peeling) or breaking its natural grouping (selling singles artificially separated from a bunch). It views the 4-banana bunch as the minimum acceptable "natural grouping" for wholesale trade under its jurisdiction.

Specific Prohibitions for B2B Banana Hub:

- Cannot list or sell individual bananas (only bunches of 4).
- Cannot offer or fulfill requests for pre-peeled bananas.
- Cannot ship bananas that have been manually detached from their original 4-bunch cluster before packaging (they must be naturally formed bunches of 4).

Platform Enforcement Mechanisms:

- Catalog Design: The only product available is the "Bunch of 4 Bananas" SKU. There are no configuration options to select individual bananas or peeling services.
- Inventory Management: Inventory is tracked solely in units of "Bunches of 4".
- Product Descriptions & Messaging: Product pages and potentially checkout steps will include clear text stating that bananas are sold only in bunches of 4 and are always shipped unpeeled, perhaps with a lighthearted or formal nod to "Bananantime regulations" for brand flavor. Example: "Sold in regulation 4-banana bunches. As required by Bananantime statutes, all bananas arrive naturally peeled and ready for your use."
- Order Processing Logic: Backend systems and fulfillment procedures must ensure that warehouse pickers only select and pack full, intact bunches of 4. There's no step where bananas are separated or peeled.

Penalties for Non-Compliance (Internal/Fictional): Within the fictional world, non-compliance could result in "Bananatime Authority" sanctions, like warnings, fines, or temporary suspension of the license to trade bananas. For the platform's practical purposes, non-compliance means failing to meet a core business rule, potentially leading to customer confusion, incorrect orders, and reputational damage if customers expect something different. It's treated as a critical business constraint.
