## Target Audience

The platform exclusively serves Business-to-Business (B2B) clients. This includes:

- Retail Grocery Chains & Independent Grocers
  - Needs: Predictable supply, potentially scheduled/recurring orders, volume discounts, dedicated account management, potentially API integration for ordering.
  - Platform Tailoring: Features like saved quick-order templates, subscription/recurring order options, tiered pricing visibility, potentially a dedicated account manager dashboard.
- Restaurants & Cafes
- Catering Companies
- Juice & Smoothie Bars
- Hotels & Hospitality Services
- Corporate & Institutional Cafeterias
- Food Service Providers
  - Needs: Reliable quality, flexible ordering, easy re-ordering, clear communication on delivery windows.
  - Platform Tailoring: Emphasis on user-friendly interface, robust order history, simple re-order functionality, clear stock level indicators.
- Small Cafes, Juice Bars, Caterers ordering for specific events
  - Needs: Ease of use for occasional orders, simple checkout, access to standard pricing, clear product information.
  - Platform Tailoring: Focus on a very intuitive onboarding and checkout process, clear display of the 4-bunch minimum and pricing, mobile-friendly design for orders on the go.
- Specialty Needs (Potential Future Segment - e.g., Organic Retailers, Bakeries needing specific ripeness):
  - Needs: Specific product attributes (organic certification, specific ripeness levels).
  - Platform Tailoring: Advanced filtering options (once variations exist), detailed product specifications, potentially certifications displayed prominently.
